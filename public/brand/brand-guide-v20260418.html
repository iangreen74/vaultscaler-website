01 — Brand Architecture

Two brands, one family.

VaultScaler is the parent company. Forgewing is the product. They share a foundation but each has its own personality. VaultScaler is the steady hand; Forgewing is the sharp edge.

Parent Company

VaultScaler

A SaaS company building automated software solutions for deep tech and consumers. VaultScaler's brand is restrained and confident — corporate credibility without corporate stuffiness.

Typography: DM Sans only · Colors: Navy + White
Product

Forgewing

A full-lifecycle agentic engineering system. Users describe what to build in plain language. Forgewing designs, codes, deploys, monitors, and resolves — autonomously. Built solid, moves fast.

Typography: DM Serif Display (display/headings) + DM Sans (body) · Full palette

05 — Tone of Voice

Confident, not corporate.

Both brands share a modern, approachable voice. We respect our audience's intelligence without drowning them in jargon. The anvil is our metaphor: we build things that last and ship them fast.

Clear over clever

Say what the product does in plain terms. Technical credibility comes from clarity, not complexity.

✓ "Describe what to build. Forgewing handles the rest." ✗ "Leverage our AI-powered paradigm for synergistic development workflows."

Confident, not arrogant

We know what we've built is good. We don't need to shout about it or tear others down.

✓ "Forgewing deploys to production AWS infrastructure autonomously." ✗ "Unlike other tools, we're actually capable of real deployment."

Human-scale

We're building powerful automation, but we talk to people like people. Short sentences. Active voice. Real words.

✓ "Your SaaS is live. Forgewing is monitoring it." ✗ "Deployment completion status has been achieved and continuous observability has been initiated."

Built solid, moves fast

This is the brand's core tension. Emphasize reliability and speed in equal measure — the anvil has wings.

✓ "Production-grade from the first deploy." ✗ "Move fast and break things."

07 — Spacing & Clear Space

Give it room to breathe.

Maintain a minimum clear space around all logos equal to the height of the "F" in Forgewing or the "V" in the VS mark. Never crowd the mark with text, borders, or other visual elements.

Logo Don'ts
✗ Don't rotate or skew the marks
✗ Don't change the brand colors
✗ Don't add drop shadows or effects
✗ Don't place on busy photo backgrounds
✗ Don't recreate — always use source files
✗ Don't use Ani at sizes below 32px (use favicon)
