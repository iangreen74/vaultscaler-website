What Is VaultScaler?

VaultScaler is a secure orchestration platform for AI models running at the edge. Whether you're operating in industrial plants, smart city infrastructure, healthcare clinics, or surveillance networks, VaultScaler gives you the control plane to deploy, monitor, and evolve intelligent agents in real time — even in fully offline environments.

Unlike cloud-dependent platforms, VaultScaler works wherever your devices are. With lightweight agents, secure OTA delivery, and full-stack observability, VaultScaler empowers organizations to operationalize edge AI without compromise.

Modular Architecture

  • Edge Agent: Lightweight runtime with model execution, health reporting, and local logging.
  • Control Plane: Web-based dashboard and CLI for deployment, updates, and fleet status.
  • OTA Channels: Push signed updates with full rollback support and update tracks (beta/stable).
  • Observability Engine: Real-time metrics, logs, model drift detection, and audit trail.
  • VaultScaler SDK: For teams building their own control layers — tap into our orchestration logic directly.

How It Works

  1. Install the VaultScaler agent on any edge device (Jetson, x86, Pi...)
  2. Push models and configs via VaultScaler CLI or dashboard
  3. Device runs AI workloads, logs inference events, reports health
  4. Trigger OTA updates, rollback, or monitor drift — in real time
