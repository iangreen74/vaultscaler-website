# Forgewing Page — Layout Skeletons

The page rotates through 5 layout skeletons to create an editorial, print-layout feel. **Never use the same skeleton in two adjacent sections.** Every image gets a short serif-italic caption.

## Skeleton 1 — Bleed Hero
Image escapes the container edge and sits higher than the headline baseline. Narrow text column anchored opposite. Caption under the image, right-aligned.
**Used in:** Hero

## Skeleton 2 — Full Bleed Backdrop
Image fills the section edge-to-edge (no container). Headline + short copy sit in a corner overlay. Tiny caption in the opposite corner.
**Used in:** *(reserved — unused so far)*

## Skeleton 3 — Marginalia
Oversized serif headline dominates. A small image (100–160px) sits like a printer's ornament beside the headline. Body copy below at normal width.
**Used in:** Hard core engineering

## Skeleton 4 — Asymmetric Split
~40/60 split. Image fills its side and bleeds to the page edge on that side only. Text fills the other side. Caption sits under the text column, not the image — reads as a margin note.
**Used in:** Night Owl

## Skeleton 5 — Inline Figure
Headline full width. Short intro paragraph. Centered image with serif-italic caption directly below. Rest of copy continues underneath. The quiet, book-like skeleton.
**Used in:** Let your vision take flight

## Imageless sections
On a Mission, What Forgewing Can Build, FAQ, CTA — typographic treatment, no image.
