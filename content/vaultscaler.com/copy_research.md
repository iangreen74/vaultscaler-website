**Current Research Questions:**

• What are the fundamental capacity bounds of multi-GPU topologies under realistic communication constraints?
• How do compression and sketching strategies shift the I/O/compute efficiency frontier?
• Can entropy-aware scheduling algorithms reduce tail latencies in swarm inference fabrics?
• What are the theoretical limits of model parallelism in bandwidth-constrained environments?

**Research Programs:**

• **Theoretical Foundations**: Mathematical models for distributed AI system limits and capacity bounds
• **Orchestration Algorithms**: Novel scheduling approaches for heterogeneous GPU clusters
• **Information Theory**: Applying channel capacity theory to AI workload distribution  
• **Swarm Intelligence**: Emergent coordination patterns in large-scale AI deployments
• **Economic Models**: Resource allocation and pricing mechanisms for compute markets

We maintain active collaborations with leading universities and publish all findings in open research venues. Our work directly informs the development of Radix, our open-source research platform.
